01 Method

How Every Source Is Scored

Integrity prevails. Five integrity criteria carry 75 points. Five criteria of fit with this reader carry 25 points. A small portfolio modifier of up to 5 points in either direction judges what the source adds to the collection as a whole.

Integrity: 75 Points

Fit With This Reader: 25 Points

Score = Integrity (0 to 75) + Fit (0 to 25) + Portfolio Modifier (minus 5 to plus 5), kept between 0 and 100.

Integrity Bands

A

62 to 75

Structurally independent, open about money and methods, accurate and fair.

B

55 to 61

Strong on most criteria, with one known limit such as a commercial owner or a declared viewpoint.

C

47 to 54

Useful and often excellent, but perspectival, institutional or thinly disclosed. Read it against others.

D

Below 47

State linked, owner dependent, opaque or closed. Kept for what it shows, read with care.

Portfolio Modifier: Plus or Minus 5 Points

Points Added

When a source covers an underserved geography, fills a disciplinary gap such as hazard science, landscape architecture or cartography, or adds ownership diversity through a cooperative, reader funded or community owned model.

Points Removed

When a source is largely redundant with a stronger one, adds prestige without perspective, survives only as an archive, or mainly aggregates the work of others.

Worked Example

What these scores are, and are not. Each score is a judgement made by an AI model against the published rubric and a set of fixed anchor sources, using each organization's own about, funding and standards pages wherever they could be read on 1 October 2026. They are not audits. A declared viewpoint lowers the non bias score even when the work is honest and accurate; it is credited under transparency and diversity instead. Where an owner or funder could not be confirmed, the entry says Unconfirmed. Nothing from the reader's original lists was removed: low scorers stay in the directory, labelled.

From Intake Form to Verified Directory

Information Intake Form

The reader answered thirty questions on purpose, geography, languages, time, topics, formats, ownership and blind spots. The answers became the specification for every later step.

Perplexity AI Deep Research

The profile was submitted to Perplexity's Deep Research mode, which returned a citation grounded set of outlets organized by region and topic.

ChatGPT Extended Thinking Deep Research

A second, independent pass added niche journals, digital native outlets and sources outside English that the first pass missed.

Claude Opus 4.6 Extended: First Synthesis

Claude merged the research reports with the reader's own reading lists into a first scored directory.

Claude Opus 5.5: Audit and Third Edition

A full audit restored 50 sources that the first deduplication had dropped, removed entries that could not be identified as real outlets, and added hazard science, disaster risk, urban planning and landscape architecture to the priority themes.

Integrity First Rescoring: Fourth Edition

At the reader's direction the weights were rebuilt so that integrity carries 75 of 100 points. Every source was rescored on the five integrity criteria against fixed anchors, and organizations were added to widen the range of regions, disciplines and viewpoints, including credible voices right of centre.

Live Verification

Every link, and each organization's about and funding pages, was requested from an open network on 1 October 2026. Closures, changes of owner and moved newsrooms found on the sources' own pages were applied. Closed outlets are labelled and kept as archives.

04 Reader Profile

The Reader This Ecosystem Was Built For

A multilingual humanitarian cartographer based in Montreal, working at the intersection of geospatial analysis, disaster risk and open data, trained in the Swiss tradition where architecture, landscape architecture, urbanism and geography are taught together.

Integrity Comes First

Independence, transparency, non bias, quality and diversity of perspectives outrank everything else. Wire services, public interest newsrooms and official statistics establish what happened; everything else is read against them.

Geographic Priorities

Canada and Quebec daily, Taiwan and the Traditional Chinese world in depth, then Switzerland, the United Kingdom and France, Japan, Southeast Asia, Central Asia and Russia, North and West Africa, Barbados and the Caribbean, Brazil and Latin America, the Pacific, and Indigenous and Arctic peoples.

Priority Themes

Hazard science, disaster risk reduction and resilience, humanitarian systems, mapping and symbolic systems, urban planning, landscape architecture and architecture, AI and technology, labour and SDG 8, climate, public policy, global political economy, arts and digital humanities.

A Range of Viewpoints

The collection sets left against right, institutions against their critics and capitals against peripheries, each source labelled for its orientation, so that no single model of journalism or school of thought dominates.

Five Working Languages

English and French every day. Traditional Chinese for depth on Taiwan. Japanese at an intermediate level with learner friendly news. Spanish occasionally at about B1. Arabic and Russian through translation and multilingual services.

No Sensationalism

Engagement bait, verification light content and false balance are scored down. The ecosystem rewards evidence, method and nuance, and it represents competing views fairly without collapsing into empty centrism.

05 Rationale

Why Curating Your Information Intake Is a Deliberate Act

Every media diet is an architecture of attention. Most people inherit theirs from algorithms, geography and habit. The feeds they scroll and the newsletters that pile up were not chosen so much as accumulated, shaped by recommendation systems whose incentive is engagement, not understanding.

This project starts from the premise that how you read matters as much as what you read. A deliberately built ecosystem is not a luxury for the overinformed. It is a precondition for thinking clearly about a complex world, and an act of intellectual self governance.

The architecture serves explicit commitments: independence over reach, transparency over prestige, verification over virality, and a range of perspectives that does not shrink the world to a handful of capitals or one side of an argument. Wire services set the factual baseline. Investigative nonprofits hold power to account. Specialist outlets go deeper than any generalist can. Research and official sources let the reader check claims against evidence.

This is not a ranking of the best media in the world. Three quarters of each score measures integrity, by a rubric anyone can inspect and dispute; one quarter measures fit with one reader's needs. Treated this way, information literacy becomes a practice: conscious, structured, multilingual and open to revision.
